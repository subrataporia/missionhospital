Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about planning your visit, consultations, medical visa support and services for international patients.

International patient support

Need help planning your care journey? Send our team an enquiry and we’ll guide you on the next steps.

Send an enquiry
Can I speak with a doctor before travelling?

Pre-arrival tele-consultations or second-opinion requests can be made through the International Patient Desk. The team can explain the process and help coordinate a request with the appropriate doctor.

What medical records should I share?

Share relevant reports, investigation results, prescriptions and a brief medical history when requested. The team will guide you on how to provide records securely; please do not send sensitive medical documents through an unsecured enquiry form.

Can you help with a medical visa invitation letter?

Contact the International Patient Desk to ask about a Visa Invitation Letter (VIL) and supporting documentation. Visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities, and patients should confirm current requirements with the appropriate embassy or consulate.

Is airport pickup available for international patients?

Airport reception and transfer support may be arranged for international patients. Please confirm availability, eligibility, pickup details and any booking requirements with the team before finalizing your travel plans.

Can I get language interpretation during my visit?

Interpretation support can be requested in advance. Tell the team your preferred language—such as Arabic, French, Russian, Bengali or Swahili—so they can confirm what support is available for your visit.

Can you help arrange accommodation and special meals?

The team can guide you about nearby partner accommodation and available dietary options. Please share your preferences early and confirm current availability and arrangements directly with the hospital.

How do payments and international insurance work?

Contact the team to discuss accepted payment arrangements, foreign currency or wire transfer guidance, and coordination with your insurer. Coverage and reimbursement depend on your insurance policy and provider; please verify them before treatment.

What should I do if I need urgent medical attention?

Do not use this enquiry form for emergencies. Contact local emergency services or call The Mission Hospital directly at +91 86875 00500.
