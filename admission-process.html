Patient & Family Guide

Admission Process

A clear guide to registration, admission, room options and billing at The Mission Hospital, Durgapur.

Explore the admission guide
A message to patients and families

At The Mission Hospital, Durgapur, the guiding belief is “Service to man is service to God.” Every patient deserves the best treatment and attention from every member of the hospital team. This guide brings together the hospital's usual routines and procedures to help patients, family members, visitors and staff understand how to make a hospital stay work smoothly.

Before your stay

Registration & admission

Registration is required before OPD or indoor treatment. The published guide lists a one-time registration fee of Rs. 100; confirm the current fee with the hospital. Reception is on the ground floor and is open round the clock. Carry your registration document and show it at Reception whenever requested.

01

Consultant's advice

If your Consultant advises admission, complete the required formalities at the ground-floor Admission Counter. During an exigency, the Emergency Department Medical Officer may assess you and recommend admission.

02

Admission formalities

The team may ask questions to help provide appropriate care. The guide says the usual procedure, intended to prevent future complications, should take about 30 minutes. Your cooperation is appreciated.

03

Expected stay & advance

Your treating Consultant will estimate the tentative length of stay. Based on this, an advance payment is made at the Admission Desk. The published guide invites patients to ask Reception, either before or during admission, for approximate treatment, hospital and investigation costs to help with planning and decisions.

04

Corporate / insurance admission

If an empanelled company, organisation or TPA will settle the account, bring an authorisation letter clearly confirming financial responsibility. The organisation determines the treatment category; items outside approved coverage may be billed directly. The source guide says an authorisation may also be faxed to the hospital CEO. If a package needs finalising, the hospital may arrange a detailed discussion.

Where to go: Reception and the Admission Counter are on the ground floor. For emergency admission, approach the Emergency Department.

Room amenities & allocation

The guide lists TV, refrigerator, telephone, internet and personal attendant services for Private, Deluxe and Deluxe Suite accommodation, and says the hospital has central air-conditioning. Beds, rooms and suites are subject to availability and allocated first come, first served. Ask the Admission Desk or floor coordinator for a room change; requests are accommodated as soon as possible, subject to availability.

Plan your payments

Billing & payment

Payment during your stay

After the advance, the guide recommends periodic interim payments in consultation with Billing to keep the account updated. It lists these payment modes:

  • Cash
  • Mastercard / Visa credit card
  • Mastercard / Visa / Maestro debit card
  • Demand draft in favour of The Mission Hospital
  • Cheque only for corporates with an MOU; personal cheques are not accepted per the guide
  • Online fund transfer: contact the Billing In-charge on the 6th floor for details

For online services, the guide asks patients to contact Billing personnel. Confirm accepted modes and current procedures before making payment.

Final bill & discharge

At discharge, Billing consolidates charges for medicines, consumables and services used during the stay. Allow reasonable time after the doctor signs the discharge order. Insured and corporate bills are handled separately; collect the No Due and Gate Pass from Billing before leaving.

  • The guide lists 12 noon as checkout time.
  • It estimates at least 3–4 hours for discharge after advice; insurance / TPA cases may depend on authorisation.
  • If blood or blood products were used, the guide requests donation / replacement before final bill settlement.
  • Submit the Visiting Card issued at admission to Billing at final payment.
  • Check the complete document before leaving the counter.

After admission, the guide directs all indoor-patient financial transactions to the Billing Department on the 6th floor.

Billing information

The published page lists 0343-398241 and 9002000736, extension 609, and fax 0343-2532550 for 24-hour billing information. The source may contain outdated or mistyped contact details; please verify current numbers with the hospital.

Insurance & corporate desks

The guide lists the Insurance / TPA desk on the ground floor, 10 am–6 pm except Sundays and holidays. Corporate queries or sanction: Corporate Desk, ground floor, extension 130. Confirm current hours and extensions before visiting.

Surgery packages

The guide describes packages for Cardiac surgery, Uro-surgery, Orthopedics, Spine, ENT and procedures such as Angioplasty. It states that planned surgery packages require full advance payment by mutual agreement and that, once agreed, the package price does not escalate even if actual costs rise. For emergencies, it says payment is due within 24 hours of admission or earlier. Package availability, terms and current payment requirements must be confirmed with the hospital before proceeding.

The guide notes that timely bill payment helps the process run smoothly.

During your stay

Facilities & mutual responsibilities

The hospital describes the inpatient as its guest and says a skilled team works around the clock to make the stay comfortable and support a safe recovery. Patient and family cooperation helps the team.

Medical treatment

Your admitting Consultant is responsible for inpatient treatment and arranges laboratory / radiology tests, medicines, diet and other necessary treatment. Tell the nurse or Resident Doctor about medicines taken before admission; the Consultant must approve their use during the stay.

Team of professionals

In-house RMOs help coordinate between the Consultant and service providers and carry out the Consultant's instructions around the clock. The hospital's skilled nursing staff provide professional care, administer medicines and treatment, support recovery and respond to emergencies. Use the Call Button to get a nurse's attention. Ward personnel assist with cleanliness and stretcher / wheelchair movement; kitchen staff provide prescribed diets. Cooperation helps all teams serve you better.

Patient consent

Besides consent at admission, special procedures advised by your Consultant require additional consent. Nursing staff will bring the relevant form to you.

Housekeeping & linen

Clothing and linen for daily changes are provided and their use is compulsory under the inpatient protocol. For linen concerns, contact the sister-in-charge or coordinator of your floor.

Money & valuables

The hospital guide says it does not accept responsibility for patients' money or valuables. Please bring as few valuables as possible.

Mobile phone use

The published guide prohibits mobile phone use by patients and asks visitors to restrict mobile use near patients. Follow current ward instructions from hospital staff.

Medicines & consumables

Medicines, surgical items and other consumables prescribed by your Consultant are supplied by the in-house pharmacy. The guide says patients sign a memo so these can be included in the bill.

Smoke-free hospital

Smoking is illegal in the hospital under applicable law, as stated in the published guide.

Maintenance support

For TV, AC, power points, nurse-call buttons, toilet plumbing, telephone and similar service needs, contact your nurse. The maintenance staff are available on call and preventive maintenance is done daily. The guide asks patients to give about 45 minutes' lead time for specific requirements.

Waste management

The hospital says it maintains a clean, disinfected environment by separating biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste and disposing of each appropriately. Patients and visitors are asked to help minimise waste.

Parking

The guide says paid parking is available at the rear of the hospital. Park in an orderly manner. Do not park in front of the hospital, as the area must remain clear for ambulances. Contact the security personnel outside if you need help.

Please verify current information

This page covers the body information published in the hospital's admission guide. Fees, tariffs, contact numbers, office hours, payment options, policies and availability may change; confirm them directly with the hospital before relying on them.
