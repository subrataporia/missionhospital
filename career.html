Frequently Asked Questions

Explore answers to common questions about career opportunities, job applications, eligibility, recruitment, and professional growth at The Mission Hospital.

Book a 15 min call

If you any questions, just book a 15-minute call with us before any doubts.

Request a call back

You can explore current job openings on our Careers page and follow the application instructions provided for the position that matches your qualifications and experience.

Career opportunities may include medical professionals, nursing and patient care, allied health and diagnostics, administration, and hospital operations, depending on current requirements.

The required qualifications vary by position. Medical, nursing, and allied healthcare roles may require relevant degrees, registrations, certifications, or professional experience.

Eligibility for freshers depends on the role and its requirements. Please review each vacancy carefully to check whether candidates with limited or no professional experience can apply.

Internship, training, and fellowship opportunities may be available for selected disciplines based on current programmes and institutional requirements. Please contact the hospital for confirmation.

Depending on the position, you may need to submit your updated CV or resume, educational certificates, professional registration details, experience certificates, and other relevant documents.

If you have already applied, you can contact the hospital's HR or recruitment team through the official contact channels and provide your application details for assistance.

Shortlisted candidates may be contacted using the contact details provided in their application. Please ensure that your phone number and email address are accurate and accessible.
