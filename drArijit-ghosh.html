Overview

Dr. Arijit Ghosh is one of the Best Orthopedic and Joint Replacement Surgeons in Durgapur, West Bengal. He is a senior consultant Orthopedic Specialist in Mission Hospital, Durgapur. Dr. Ghosh has an experience of more than 15 Years in this field. His special field of interest includes Primary and Revision Joint Replacement, Complex trauma including Periprosthetic fractures, Sports Medicine and arthroscopy, Hand and wrist trauma and disorders as Best Bone & Joint Doctor in Durgapur. Dr. Ghosh is also trained in Advanced Trauma Life Support which makes him the Best Trauma Doctor in West Bengal. His other services include knee, shoulder and ankle arthroscopy and is the Best Arthroscopic Surgeon in Durgapur, West Bengal.
